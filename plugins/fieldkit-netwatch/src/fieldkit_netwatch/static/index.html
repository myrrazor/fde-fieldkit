Evidence contract

  • StoredDestination, port, protocol, local service identification, decision, timing, byte totals, and sanitized plaintext paths.
  • Never storedHeaders, bodies, credentials, URL queries, prompts, Bash commands, search queries, or command arguments.
  • TLSHTTPS paths and payloads remain encrypted unless a supported hook reports a sanitized target.
  • Blind spotsDNS packets, UDP/QUIC, direct sockets that bypass proxies, and short-lived sockets missed between attach samples.
  • Whole machineUniversal arbitrary-process enforcement requires a separately signed macOS Network Extension.
