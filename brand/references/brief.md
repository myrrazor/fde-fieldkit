# Field Kit logo brief

- Audience: forward-deployed engineers working with data, drafts and AI workloads.
- Advantage: eight focused tools that run locally with explicit network consent.
- Personality: precise, practical and calm.
- Desired feeling: a dependable instrument within easy reach.
- Avoid: bevels, parchment tiles, double borders, textures, generic terminals,
  sparkles and monograms.

Recognition hook: opposing caliper jaws with a central sliding connector. Keep
that silhouette; remove the enclosing tile and ornamental construction.

The original source is preserved as `source-caliper.png`. The image-generation
study explores reduction; final SVG geometry is drawn deliberately from scratch
and is not traced from the study.

## Image study prompt

Create one minimal, compact caliper symbol derived from the attached source.
Preserve the opposed measuring jaws and central connection as the recognition
hook. Remove the enclosing rounded tile, parchment, outlines, bevels, scratches
and every small mechanical detail. Use two or three broad geometric shapes,
near-square visible proportions and generous negative space. A deep charcoal
body and one small muted brick-red connection only. Flat vector-like fills, no
live strokes, shadows, gradients, texture, perspective or lighting. Center the
text-free symbol on a flat vivid turquoise background for silhouette evaluation.
No lettering, monograms, terminals, sparkles, mockups or additional objects.
Make the symbol readable at favicon sizes and balanced beside the name Field Kit.
